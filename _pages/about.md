I am a researcher at [Luma AI](https://lumalabs.ai), working on agentic robotics, with the long-term goal of building general-purpose agents that can perceive, reason, and act in the real world.

Previously, I was at ByteDance Seed, where I worked on text-to-audio generation and was a founding member of Seed Music. Before joining ByteDance, I received my Ph.D. from Duke University under the supervision of [Prof. Lawrence Carin](https://ece.duke.edu/people/lawrence-carin/). My doctoral research focused on vision-language navigation (VLN), knowledge distillation, and information theory.

## Research Interests

My recent research focuses on:

- **Agentic Robotics**: embodied agents that plan, use tools, and act in the physical world
- **Reinforcement Learning**: learning from interaction and feedback for decision making and model post-training
- **Multimodal Understanding and Generation**: unified models that understand and generate across vision, language, and other modalities
- **Audio Generation**: generative models for speech, sound, and music
